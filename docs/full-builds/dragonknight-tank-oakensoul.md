# Dragonknight Hybrid Tank — "Accolade" (One Bar, Oakensoul)
### The tank that plays the rest of the game — solo world bosses, vet solo arenas, and a soloed normal trial

**Character:** Dragonknight, **pure class** (no subclassing — Class Mastery is a big part of this build)
**Verified against:** The Tank Club's *Accolade* Dragonknight Hybrid Tank Build (2026), transcribed from the author's own video walkthrough on 2026-09-14. Skill lines and set bonuses cross-checked against ESO-Hub/UESP (U50). This is **his** build, reproduced — not a household construction. Items marked ⚠️ are where the transcript was ambiguous and need confirming in-game.
**Playstyle:** One bar, Sword & Board, no bar swapping. Taunt, debuff, chain things in, and do genuinely respectable damage while being very hard to kill.

---

## What this is — and what it isn't

The author is unusually clear about the trade, and it's worth repeating before you spend gold on it:

> *"You're not going to hit like a fully optimized damage dealer. Some fights will take longer. You need patience. But in return, you'll gain consistency, survivability, control and the freedom to experience almost all of ESO as a tank."*

**Designed for:** solo world bosses, veteran solo arenas, veteran pledges and dungeons, base-game hard-mode dungeons, **normal trials**, Overland Challenge Mode, gear farming, carrying groups with poor DPS.

**Not designed for:** veteran hard-mode trials or dungeons in an optimised group. It does not replace a dedicated main tank build.

!!! warning "The soloed trial is never named"
    The author states the build "soloed a 12-player trial" but **does not say which trial or at what difficulty**, and the video isn't reachable from here to check. He describes the build's ceiling as "base game hard mode dungeons, or normal trials," so a **normal** trial is the reasonable reading.

    That matters, because most trials are hard-gated against solo play by mechanics rather than damage — Aetherian Archive's pressure plates need the full group, Cloudrest's portals need at least two players, Hall of Fabrication's upstairs triggers need four. No amount of tankiness opens those. **Normal Asylum Sanctorium** is the usual answer for a genuine solo trial clear, because Saint Olms has no DPS check — but that's inference, not something the author said.

## Skills — One Bar (Sword & Board)

| # | Skill | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goading Throw**<br>*scribed grimoire — Shield Throw, One Hand and Shield*<br>⚠️ scripts: Taunt / Lingering Torment / Maim | Single-target damage, a DoT, **the taunt**, and a 10% enemy damage reduction. The most efficient taunt available here because you already have Major and Minor Breach from elsewhere. |
| 2 | **Burning Embers**<br>*morph of Searing Strike, Ardent Flame* | Flex slot. Direct flame damage, a flame DoT, a **burst heal plus a heal over time**, and the Burning status. | 3 | **Chains of Dominance**<br>*morph of Chains of Flame, Draconic Power* | The build's engine. Pulls adds in; applies **Major Cowardice**; taunts anything untaunted; applies Burning; procs Combustion; gives the group **Minor Brutality**. |
| 4 | **Razor Caltrops**<br>*morph of Caltrops, Alliance War → Assault* | **Major Breach** — your core tank responsibility. AoE DoT and a slow, and it's what **activates Powerful Assault** for you and the group. |
| 5 | **Burning Talons**<br>*morph of Dark Talons, Draconic Power* | Immobilise, surprisingly high flame damage, another flame DoT, and a group synergy. Crowd control *and* one of your best AoE damage tools. |
| **Ult** | **Corrosive Armor**<br>*Earthen Heart* | AoE flame damage, **completely ignores enemy armour while active**, and **caps incoming damage at 6% of Max Health**. Slotting it also unlocks **Mountain's Blessing**, so heavy attacks restore more Stamina and set enemies Off Balance. |

### Swaps the author calls out

- **Slot 2 on add pulls → Hearth and Home** (*morph of Hearthfire, Ardent Flame*) — restores Magicka and Stamina, heals, and bursts AoE damage. *(It also grants Major Protection, which Oakensoul does not provide.)*
- **Slot 2 while levelling → Engulfing Dragonfire** (*morph of Dragonfire Breath, Draconic Power*) — good for chewing through many enemies, but it's a channel, so it's a poor fit for tanky content where you need to block on reaction.
- **Slot 5 if Talons drains you → Binding Burst** (*scribed grimoire — Soul Burst, Soul Magic*; scripts: Immobilize / Lingering Torment / Breach). Keeps the immobilise, gives a 20-second DoT so you recast far less, and adds **AoE Minor Breach**. Noticeably less damage.
- **Ultimate → Standard of Might** (*morph of Dragonknight Standard, Ardent Flame*) for group content — more group damage, more damage reduction, and an AoE synergy. The author rates it clearly **worse than Corrosive Armor when solo**.
- **Ultimate → Shooting Star** (*morph of Meteor, Mages Guild*) for damage-focused solo play — flame damage, a flame DoT, and it **refunds Ultimate for every enemy hit** on the first hit, which is excellent in solo arenas and add-heavy fights. Brings Mages Guild's max Magicka and Magicka recovery passives with it.

### Class Mastery (pure class — pick 2)

- **Resolute Defense** — while blocking, block mitigation climbs **6% per second up to 30%**, and blocking has a chance to restore 500 Stamina. This is what makes holding block through a bad phase realistic.
- **Lead from the Front** — using an Ultimate grants **Major Berserk and Major Protection to you and your group**. Stacks enormous value onto every ultimate cast, which pairs directly with War Machine.

## How to play it

**Boss fights** — there's an actual rotation here:

1. Open with **Goading Throw** (taunt + DoT)
2. **Burning Embers** for the DoT and the healing
3. **Razor Caltrops** underneath the boss
4. **Burning Talons**
5. **Chains of Dominance ×3–4** — see below, this is free damage
6. Refresh **Talons**, then start again

Weave light attacks between casts and block heavies and mechanics on reaction. On a nasty boss, slow the rotation down and prioritise survival over uptime.

!!! tip "Why Chains is spammed on bosses"
    **When you chain an enemy that can't be pulled, the Magicka cost is refunded.** Against a boss that means Chains is effectively free — free damage, free Burning, free Combustion procs for sustain, and it keeps Major Cowardice up. That's the single most important trick in the build.

**Add pulls** — the order matters:

1. Taunt the priority targets first — elites, and anything holding a two-hander
2. Throw **Caltrops in front** of the group so they walk into it
3. Stand in the middle of your own Caltrops
4. **Talons** to lock everything down
5. **Chain** the archers and mages in, on top of the Caltrops
6. **Talons** again to lock the new arrivals, and pick up any remaining taunts

**When resources get low:** spend your Ultimate (it refunds resources through the Dragonknight's ultimate-use passive ⚠️ — the transcript garbles the passive's name), and use the **"heavy after a heavy"** trick — land a heavy attack immediately after the boss lands one on you. That's the safest window to drop block and recover.

*The Archdruid piece can be **either** the helm or the shoulder — take whichever you already own. The author suggests Powerful Assault on weapons and jewellery (most tanks own those already) and War Machine on armour (easier to farm), but the split is flexible: any arrangement that reaches 5 and 5 works. Jewellery is 1 Triune + 2 Infused, aiming for **30k max Health as a floor** before chasing anything else — go 3 Infused if you'd rather. In-game tooltips override.*

!!! note "Why the weapon enchant is Absorb Stamina"
    It's not for sustain — it's to proc **Sundered** consistently, which applies **Minor Breach** and shreds more armour. That's a tank responsibility the build picks up for free from an enchant.

**Mundus:** **The Thief.** The author tested The Shadow and found the extra crit damage worth less than the crit chance here.
**Attributes:** **all 64 into Magicka** — it raises damage *and* carries the Chains spam and self-healing through solo fights.
**Food:** **Bewitched Sugar Skulls.** Recovery-focused foods were tested and performed worse.
**Potions:** standard tri-stat tank potions. The build already has Major Prophecy from Oakensoul, so spell power potions are wasted — spend the slot on sustain.
**Race:** **Nord** if you might ever take this toward veteran hard modes (resistances, max stats, ultimate generation — the resistances buy you freedom in traits and CP). **Imperial** is the stronger pick for the content this build actually targets, for max stats and cheaper abilities.

## Before you build this — household notes

These are the things that apply to **our** characters specifically, and they're not in the video.

- **This is his build, not hers.** Slot 1 is a **scribed** grimoire, and so is the slot-5 alternative. She doesn't scribe, and there's no non-scribed replacement for the taunt that keeps the same utility — so this sheet isn't portable to her.
- **Razor Caltrops gates the whole thing.** It unlocks through **Alliance War → Assault**, which means ranking up in Cyrodiil or Battlegrounds. That's the same gate flagged on her [Stamina Sorcerer](../one-bar-builds/one-bar-sorcerer-stamina.md) — if you grind Assault once, two builds benefit. Without it you have no Major Breach, and the build loses a core tank responsibility.
- **Archdruid Devyric is a stat stick here, nothing more.** Its **Major Vulnerability is the 2-item bonus**; the 1-item is 1,487 Offensive Penetration. Oakensoul occupies the mythic slot, so only one monster piece fits — exactly the [12-slot math](../shared/gear-math.md) every other character here runs into. **Do not expect Major Vulnerability from this setup.** (The two-bar version of Accolade drops Oakensoul, which frees the slot for the full 2-piece — that's where the Major Vulnerability claim belongs.)
- **You only get two Undaunted Mettle tiers.** 6 Medium + 1 Heavy is two distinct weights, so you're claiming 4% rather than 6% of max Health/Magicka/Stamina. ⚠️ **Worth checking whether War Machine drops in Light** — if it does, one Light piece is +2% to all three pools for almost nothing. If both sets are weight-locked the way Deadly Strike is, it's not available.
- **This drops Ring of the Pale Order**, which is a fixture everywhere else in this repo. That's deliberate and unavoidable — Oakensoul *is* the build. Worth knowing you're giving up 20% of damage as healing to get 17 buffs and a one-bar playstyle.
- **Food and Mundus carry straight over** from the [Magicka Dragonknight](magicka-dragonknight.md) — same Bewitched Sugar Skulls, same Thief. One less thing to buy.
- **Make it an Armory loadout**, not a new character. The whole selling point is not respeccing between content types.

## Skill Line Passives — buy these with skill points

Buy every passive in every line you have a skill slotted from. For this build that's:

- **Dragonknight** — all three lines, all passives. Combustion and the ultimate-refund passive are load-bearing for sustain
- **One Hand and Shield** — all; also the line that unlocks the Shield Throw grimoire at rank 25
- **Medium Armor** — all (both five-piece sets are Medium: damage passives plus block cost reduction)
- **Heavy Armor** — all (the Archdruid piece)
- **Undaunted** — Undaunted Mettle especially
- **Alchemy** — Medicinal Use (potions last longer)
- **Racial** — all
- **Mages Guild** — only if you run the Shooting Star ultimate
