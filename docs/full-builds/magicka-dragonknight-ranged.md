# Magicka Dragonknight — Ranged (Two Staves)
### The ranged variant of the Mag DK — two inferno staves

The ranged setup for the Mag DK, for when you'd rather kite than brawl. It's ~2.3% behind the melee (dual-dagger) build on paper but plays entirely at range. **Everything — Class Mastery, the five front-bar skills, the back bar, the rotation, the Trials and PvP sections, and the full CP roadmap — is identical to the [Melee Mag DK guide](magicka-dragonknight.md); only the front-bar weapon and the armor weight change.**

## What changes vs the [Melee guide](magicka-dragonknight.md)

1. **Front bar → Inferno Staff** (ranged) instead of dual daggers. Same five skills (Molten Whip, Searing Claw, Disintegrating Dragonfire, Soul of Flame, Ulfsild's) — they're weapon-agnostic. The back bar is an **inferno** staff, same as the melee build — see [the staff note there](magicka-dragonknight.md#gear-solo) for why it changed from Ice on 2026-09-30. Short version: Ancient Knowledge's inferno bonus is **+12% damage done with damage-over-time and Status Effects**, which is this whole build, while its ice bonus is block cost and block mitigation — worth nothing on a bar you swap off. **On this variant the case is even stronger than on the melee build**, because you're holding a staff on *both* bars, so the inferno bonus is live the entire fight instead of only while you're on the back bar.
2. **Armor → Light** (5 Light / 1 Medium / 1 Heavy) for Concentration penetration + Prodigy crit — ~2% more damage than the melee build's Medium armor, traded for survivability.
