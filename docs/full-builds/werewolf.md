# Werewolf Master Guide — Update 50
### Werewolf Berserker — Solo & Group DPS

!!! warning "The werewolf line was reworked in Update 50 — verify the bar in-game"
    U50 didn't just rename skills — it changed how the form *works*. Staying a wolf now costs **Ultimate over time** instead of running a drain timer, **Infectious Claws became Rending Claws**, **Piercing Howl became Gnash**, and the old "re-press the ultimate" burst is now the **Fury → Rampage** meter. Everything below is rewritten against the [Hyperioxes solo Werewolf build](https://hyperioxes.com/eso/solo/werewolf-build), Hack the Minotaur's U50 Werewolf guide and the U50 patch notes. A **lot** of pre-U50 werewolf material is still circulating (this page carried some of it until now), so **confirm the exact morphs on your own bar** — in-game tooltips always win.

Werewolf isn't a class — it's a transformation you slot on your existing character. Build normally, put the Werewolf Transformation ultimate on a bar, and press it to become a fast, self-healing melee beast with its own skill bar. This is the **damage** setup. For the easy/tanky version, see the [one-bar Werewolf cheat sheet](../one-bar-builds/werewolf.md).

## Morph: Werewolf Berserker

Both morphs are of **Werewolf Transformation** (*World → Werewolf*). You take **Werewolf Berserker**:

- **Major Berserk** (+damage) plus bonus **Bleed** damage on your light and heavy attacks
- **Rampage** — *not* a re-press of the ultimate. Dealing damage in form builds **Fury**; at **1,000 Fury** you trigger **Rampage**, and the Berserker version gives more damage, movement speed and **reduced ability costs**

*(Pack Leader is the defensive morph — Major Protection + two summoned Dire Wolves, and its **Enduring Rampage** adds Health Recovery. Its two wolves also cut the form's Ultimate upkeep from 100 to **36 per 10s**, which is a real reason to consider it for long solo pushes. Otherwise Berserker is the DPS pick.)*

## The werewolf form bar

When you transform you get a fixed 5-slot beast bar. Recommended slots:

1. **Feral Pounce** (morph of Pounce, *Werewolf*) — **dynamic in U50**: cast from beyond 7m it's the leaping gap-closer; cast in melee range it becomes **Feral Carnage**, a single-target DoT. Restores Stamina and builds Fury either way. Since you fight up close you'll mostly be pressing the Carnage half — treat it as a DoT you refresh, not just an engage.
    - **Feral vs Brutal is your choice of morph:** **Feral** (Pounce/Carnage) is the **single-target** version — take it for bosses and the household default. **Brutal** (Brutal Pounce / Brutal Carnage) is the **AoE** version — take it if you mostly grind trash packs and Infinite Archive.
2. **Hircine's Rage** (morph of Hircine's Bounty, *Werewolf*) — burst heal that **scales off your offensive stats** (Weapon/Spell Damage), grants up to **12% damage done for 20s** scaled to your *current* Health percentage, and grants Max Stamina. ⚠️ The catch: **it raises your damage *taken* by the same amount**, so it's a damage cooldown as much as a heal. Sources also put **Minor Berserk** on this morph — confirm on your bar.
    - **Survivability swap: Hircine's Fortitude** (the other morph) heals off **Max Health (~40%)** and grants **12–24% Max Stamina** with **no damage-taken penalty**. That's the "kick ass and not die" pick — take Fortitude the moment a fight is killing you, and keep Rage for content you're comfortably clearing.
3. **Ferocious Roar** (morph of Roar, *Werewolf*) — fears nearby enemies (sets them Off Balance), applies **Major Courage**, grants **Major Savagery & Prophecy while slotted** (your crit buff — no potion needed for it), and gives a Feeding Frenzy synergy (+6% damage, Minor Force).
4. **Claw Fury** (morph of Rending Claws, *Werewolf* — the U50 rename of Infectious Claws) — a **channeled** claw attack, ~5s cone; your main sustained damage. It genuinely **is** a channel, so **Deadly Strike's 5-piece +15% is the whole reason it's the gear set** — and the table below now actually runs 5 pieces. With Pale Order + the Reaving Blows CP star it heals you as it ticks. *(The other morph is **Bloodclaws**.)*
5. **Rip and Tear** (morph of **Gnash** — the U50 redesign of Piercing Howl, *Werewolf*) — the household default. It applies **Major Breach**, **heals you for roughly 21% of your Max Health**, and **taunts while you're blocking**. A spammable that debuffs and heals is exactly the trade this repo takes every time.
    - **Parse option: Bloody Gnash**, the other morph — Hack the Minotaur runs **Claw Fury as the channeled spammable and Bloody Gnash as an execute below 25%**. Take it only if you're chasing a number; Rip and Tear's Breach + heal is worth more in solo content.

### Two U50 mechanics that change how you press buttons

- **Blood Hunger.** **Claw Fury generates a Blood Hunger stack every second while you channel it, and deals +25% damage per stack** — so a full channel ramps hard and clipping it early throws the ramp away. **Rip and Tear consumes a stack for +25% initial damage**, and **Bloodclaws grants a stack per enemy hit**. Practically: hold Claw Fury to the end, *then* spend the stack with Rip and Tear.
- **Prowl.** Werewolf now has its own stealth. A **Pounce out of Prowl deals +50% damage and stuns** — your opener on any pack or boss you get to approach unaware.

## Staying a werewolf — it's an Ultimate cost now, not a timer

U50 deleted the old drain timer. The form runs on **Ultimate**:

- **100 Ultimate to transform**, then **100 Ultimate every 10 seconds to stay in form**.
- **Pack Leader's two direwolves cut that upkeep to 36 per 10s.** Berserker pays the full 100 — which is why Berserker is the burst-window morph and Pack Leader is the "live in wolf form" morph.
- **Devour now restores Ultimate**, not time. Hold interact on a corpse to heal *and* buy yourself more seconds of form. Snack between packs — this is your only real in-combat refill.
- Generate Ultimate the normal ways before you transform (heavy attacks, taking hits, Ultimate-generation CP/sets) and you'll hold the form far longer.

## Passives — buy all of them

*World → Werewolf.* Rule of thumb: **every werewolf passive, always.**

- **Pursuit** — more stamina recovery in form
- **Blood Rage** — builds Fury (toward Rampage) when you take/deal damage
- **Devour** — eat corpses to heal and restore Ultimate (which is what keeps you in form)
- **Savage Strength** — Weapon/Spell Damage while transformed
- **Call of the Pack** — reduces the transform cost and the form's Ultimate upkeep (stay a wolf longer)
- **Bloodmoon** — lets you infect another player at the ritual site (how you'd turn each other — below)

Buy the Fighters Guild passives too (Slayer especially).

## Gear

Werewolf can't bar-swap, so it's **one loadout**: armor + jewelry + equipped weapons' set bonuses all apply in form. The shape is **5× Deadly Strike + 5× Order's Wrath + one monster helm + Ring of the Pale Order** — the same arrangement as [her Pack Leader sheet](../one-bar-builds/werewolf.md), because it's the only one that fits.

**Deadly Strike must be 5 pieces.** Its 15% channelled/DoT bonus is the *entire* reason it's on a Claw Fury build — Claw Fury is a real channel — and at 4 pieces you get stat lines and none of the point. Earlier versions of this page tabled it at 4; that's fixed below.

**Balorgh is no longer the default.** It appears in no current U50 werewolf build, and its "scale off the Ultimate you spent" proc is a much worse bet now that Ultimate drains continuously to hold the form rather than being dumped once. Run a **single stat helm** instead (Slimecraw's 657 Crit Chance if you own it, or a penetration helm if you're under the cap).

**Armor weight: 5 Medium / 1 Light (belt) / 1 Heavy (chest)** — the split triggers all three tiers of **Undaunted Mettle** (max Health/Magicka/Stamina per *distinct* weight worn).

| Slot | Weight | Trait | Enchant | Set |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head | Medium | Divines | Stamina | **Slimecraw** **(1pc monster — 657 Crit Chance)** |
| Shoulders | Medium | Divines | Stamina | **Order's Wrath** |
| Chest | Heavy | Reinforced | Stamina | **Order's Wrath** |
| Hands | Medium | Divines | Stamina | **Order's Wrath** |
| Belt | **Light** | Divines | Stamina | **Order's Wrath** |
| Legs | Medium | Divines | Stamina | **Order's Wrath** |
| Boots | Medium | Divines | Stamina | **Deadly Strike** |
| Necklace | — | Bloodthirsty | Weapon Damage | **Deadly Strike** |
| Ring 1 | — | Bloodthirsty | Weapon Damage | **Deadly Strike** |
| Ring 2 | — | — (mythic) | — | **Ring of the Pale Order** |
| Weapons (2H or dual) | — | Nirnhoned / Charged | Weapon Damage / Poison | **Deadly Strike** (a 2H or a dual-wield pair = 2 pieces) |

*Deadly Strike = boots + necklace + ring + both weapon slots (5 — the channel buff is live). Order's Wrath = shoulders/chest/hands/belt/legs (5). Helm + mythic ring fill the last two slots.*

*Why only one monster piece: Ring of the Pale Order takes the mythic slot, and 12 gear slots only stretch to 5 + 5 + 1 monster + 1 mythic. So the helm is a **pure stat line** — Slimecraw's 2-piece Minor Berserk can never fire here. If your Offensive Penetration is under the 18,200 cap, Valkyn Skoria's 1-piece (1,487 Offensive Penetration) is the better stat. See [Gear slot math](../shared/gear-math.md). In-game tooltips override — confirm on the bar.*

**Group content:** in grouped content with a healer, drop **Pale Order** for a damage ring (complete the 3rd Deadly Strike jewelry piece) — same as every other build in this repo.

**Mundus:** The Thief (crit) → The Lover for penetration
**Attributes:** 64 Stamina (shift to Health for the nastiest fights)
**Food:** Max Health + Stamina (Orzorga's Smoked Bear Haunch)
**Race:** whatever the character already is — the spread is ~5%

## Champion Points — Spend Order (1200 → 1800)

At CP 1200 you have ~400 points per color; at 1800, ~600. **Each table is in the order you actually unlock it** — the tree opens outward from the center, so buy top to bottom and every row is reachable by the time you get to it (connector stars come before the deeper stars they gate). The **`Slot`** line names the active stars for that tree — buy those as soon as the tree lets you reach them, and fill the passives as you path through. Exact node adjacency shifts a little with which stars you pick, so glance at the in-game tree to confirm.

## How to play

1. Pre-buff on your normal bars, build to at least 100 Ultimate, then **press the transformation ultimate**.
2. **Open from Prowl if you can** — a Pounce out of stealth does **+50% damage and stuns**.
3. **Ferocious Roar** (fear, Off Balance, Major Courage, and your crit buff) → **Feral Carnage** (Pounce in melee) as a DoT.
4. **Hold Claw Fury to the end of the channel** — every second adds a **Blood Hunger** stack worth **+25% damage**, so clipping it costs you the ramp. Then **spend the stack with Rip and Tear** (+25% initial damage, plus Major Breach and a big heal).
5. **Hircine's Rage** when health dips — remembering it also raises your damage taken, so use it to heal *and* burst, not as a panic button in a fight that's already going badly. (Running Hircine's Fortitude instead? Then it *is* the panic button.)
6. **Watch your Ultimate, not a timer** — 100 per 10s keeps you a wolf. **Devour a corpse** between packs to heal and refill it.
7. **Rampage fires at 1,000 Fury** — it's a payoff for dealing damage, not a button you press. Keep swinging and it comes.

## Turning each other into werewolves

You catch Lycanthropy from a Werewolf world boss in **Bangkorai / Reaper's March / The Rift** at night, or a bite from another player. Once one of you is a werewolf, the **Bloodmoon** passive lets that person **bite the other** at the werewolf ritual site — so you only need to get infected once, then you can turn each other. Do the short Werewolf questline after infection to unlock the skill line.
