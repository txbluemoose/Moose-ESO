# Thieving & Heists — the dedicated loadout
### For the husband. Nothing on this page helps you fight anything.

This is the loadout that was "considered and rejected" in the maintenance notes for months, on the grounds that it isn't a combat build. That's still true — and it's exactly why it belongs in its own **Armory Assistant slot** rather than being compromised into a combat page. Swap to it for a thieving session, swap back before you go fight something.

The reason it's worth building now: the **[Nowhere Vault](../shared/mythics.md#prowlers-talisman-yours-but-not-for-combat)** opened in Update 51, and it holds the last **Prowler's Talisman** upgrade gem. Your trove and chest routes now pay toward finishing a mythic instead of just gold.

!!! warning "One mythic at a time"
    ESO lets you equip **one** mythic, so **Prowler's Talisman and The Shadow Queen's Cowl compete** even though they sit in different slots. The ruling from [Mythic Items](../shared/mythics.md) stands: the **Cowl is stronger for pure heist work** (see guards and witnesses through walls while crouched, pickpocketing a witness stuns them, big detection reduction), the **Talisman is free, already half-upgraded, and gives crouch-invisibility**. Finish the Talisman first because it's nearly done; dig the Cowl only if the thieving bug bites harder.

Two crafted/overland sets and a mythic. Nothing here needs a trial, a dungeon or a trader.

| Weapons | — | anything | anything | **Night's Silence** (a 2H or a staff = 2 pieces) |

*Night Mother's Embrace = 5 body. Night's Silence = belt + boots + 2 rings + the weapon (a two-hander counts as 2, so you reach 5 with one weapon). The necklace is the mythic. **All Medium, and that's deliberate** — Medium Armor's **Shadowstrike** passive reduces sneak detection radius and **Improved Sneak** cuts sneak cost, so unlike every combat page in this repo there's no Undaunted Mettle weight-split to balance. Nothing here cares about Max Magicka.*

!!! danger "Night Mother's Embrace cannot go on Light armor"
    Its slot list is **Medium, Heavy, Shield, Weapons, Necklace, Ring** — there is **no Light option**. Same trap as Deadly Strike in the combat guides: check the weight before you place a piece, or you'll craft something unwearable.

**What the two 5-pieces actually do:**

| Set | 5-piece | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Night's Silence** | **Ignore the Movement Speed penalty of Sneak entirely** | **Crafted** — stations at Hightide Keep (Auridon), Mesanthano's Tower (Glenumbra), Steamfont Cavern (Stonefalls). You craft, so this is free |
| **Night Mother's Embrace** | **Detection radius −2 metres** *and* **Sneak cost −25%**, plus +171 Weapon and Spell Damage | **Overland — Deshaan.** Not craftable; farm it or buy it cheap from a trader |

**Why both:** they solve different halves of the problem. Night's Silence makes a crouched session *fast* — crouch-walking at full speed is the single biggest quality-of-life change to a city run. Night Mother's Embrace makes you *harder to see*. If you only want one, take **Night's Silence** — speed is what makes a thieving night short, and the detection numbers are already generous once the passives and CP are in.

## Skill lines and passives — buy all of these

Rule of thumb for this page as much as the combat ones: **buy every passive in every line you have a skill slotted from.** Here the lines matter even with nothing slotted, because these are all passive.

### Legerdemain (*World*) — five passives, all of them

Levels from **laundering or fencing** (1 point each) and **lockpicking** (1–8 points by lock difficulty).

| Passive | At max |
|---|---|
| **Improved Hiding** | Reduces the cost of Sneak by **40%** |
| **Light Fingers** | Increases your chance of successfully **pickpocketing by 50%** |
| **Trafficker** | Increases the number of **fence interactions per day by 180%** |
| **Locksmith** | Improves your chance of **forcing locks by 70%** |
| **Kickback** | Reduces bounties you **willingly pay** to guards and fences by **40%** |

### Thieves Guild — five passives, and one of them is a gate

| Passive | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Finders Keepers** | **Thieves troves can only be opened by Thieves Guild members.** This is the prerequisite for the entire trove economy — without it the caches aren't lootable |
| **Haggling** | Stolen items sold at a fence are worth **10% more**. ⚠️ **Does not apply to laundering** |
| **Swiftly Forgotten** | **Bounty −115 after 3 minutes; Heat −64 after 3 seconds** |
| **Clemency** | Once per day, a guard who accosts you **won't arrest you or take your money and stolen goods** — and won't accost you again for 1 minute unless you reoffend |
| **Timely Escape** | With Bounty and in combat, a chance to spot a **Footpad** in any town with a Refuge; interacting teleports you safely into it |

!!! note "A correction while I was here"
    The [Farming page](farming.md) listed **Finders Keepers** as a *Legerdemain* passive. It's **Thieves Guild**. Two separate lines, levelled two different ways — Legerdemain from fencing, laundering and lockpicking; Thieves Guild from its own questline and dailies. Fixed on that page too.

**Also relevant:** **Medium Armor** gives **Shadowstrike** (sneak detection radius) and **Improved Sneak** (sneak cost) — both free on this loadout since it's 7/7 Medium. And if you have the **Dark Brotherhood** line, **Blade of Woe** is what the Green CP star *Shadowstrike* keys off (different thing, same unfortunate name).

## Champion Points — 🟢 GREEN (Craft)

The Green tree is where the thieving build actually lives, and the combat guides all dismiss it with "nothing here affects combat" — true, and beside the point on this page. **29 stars: 10 slottable, 19 passive.** You get **four slots**, same as the other two trees, so the slottables are a real choice.

### The four slots

| # | Star | Pts | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sustaining Shadows** | **SLOT** (50) | Sneak costs **50% less**. Stacks with Improved Hiding and Night Mother's Embrace — together, crouching becomes effectively free |
| 2 | **Steed's Blessing** | **SLOT** (50) | **+20% out-of-combat movement speed.** You're travelling between marks constantly; this is the whole session's pace |
| 3 | **Fade Away** | **SLOT** (50) | Escaping a Guard wipes **25% of your current Heat** — but *not* Bounty. The "I got spotted, now what" button |
| 4 | **Friends in Low Places** | **SLOT** (25) | Removes **1,000 gold of bounty once per day**, automatically, when you commit a crime that adds bounty. Needs level 50 and a current bounty of at least 1,000 |

*Swap options rather than fixtures:* **Shadowstrike** (75) makes a **Blade of Woe** kill turn you invisible for 5 seconds and distracts nearby witnesses — slot it over Fade Away if you're doing assassination work rather than theft. **Master Gatherer** (75, −50% harvest time) and **War Mount** (75, no mount stamina cost out of combat) belong in a *gathering* loadout, not this one.

### Passives — buy these, they're always on

| # | Star | Pts | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Out of Sight** | buy max (30) | Sneak detection radius **−3 metres** |
| 2 | **Infamous** | buy max (25) | **+25% value of fenced items.** The single biggest gold star on the tree for this loadout |
| 3 | **Fortune's Favor** | buy max (50) | **+50% gold** from **treasure chests and safeboxes** |
| 4 | **Cutpurse's Art** | buy max (25) | Higher-quality loot when **pickpocketing** |
| 5 | **Fleet Phantom** | buy max (40) | Sneak movement-speed penalty **−25%** — redundant once Night's Silence removes it entirely, so **buy it last**, or skip it while you're wearing the set |
| 6 | **Treasure Hunter** | buy max (50) | Higher-quality items from treasure chests |
| 7 | **Gilded Fingers** | buy max (50) | **+10% gold gained**, everywhere |
| 8 | **Wanderer** | buy max (50) | **−50% wayshrine cost** — you fast-travel a lot on a route |
| 9 | **Liquid Efficiency** | buy max (50) | 10% chance per stage not to consume a potion |
| 10 | **Breakfall** | buy max (50) | **−35% fall damage.** Not a joke on this page: dropping off balconies is a normal way to leave a building |
| 11 | **Homemaker** | buy max (25) | 10% chance of a second furnishing plan — free gold from a thing you're already looting |

*Everything else in Green is crafting, fishing or gathering. Good stars, wrong loadout.*

## How the stacks actually add up

This is the part worth knowing, because it's where the loadout stops being a list and starts being a different game:

| What | Sources | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Sneak cost** | Improved Hiding −40%, Sustaining Shadows −50%, Night Mother's Embrace −25%, plus Medium Armor's Improved Sneak | Effectively free — stop thinking about stamina while crouched |
| **Detection radius** | Out of Sight −3m, Night Mother's Embrace −2m, plus Medium Armor's Shadowstrike | **−5m before the armour passive.** NPCs have to be close |
| **Sneak speed** | Night's Silence removes the penalty outright | Full movement speed while crouched |
| **Fence payout** | Haggling +10%, Infamous +25% | ⚠️ Both are **fencing** bonuses. **Neither applies to laundering** — Haggling says so explicitly |
| **Chest and safebox gold** | Fortune's Favor +50%, Gilded Fingers +10% | Worth having before a trove route, not after |

**The laundering/fencing split is the one to internalise:** your +35% of payout bonuses only fire when you **fence** (sell stolen goods for gold). **Laundering** — converting a stolen item into a clean one you can keep or sell normally — gets none of it. So fence the junk, launder only what you actually want.

## What to spend the loadout on

- **Thieves troves** — needs **Finders Keepers**. The [Farming page](farming.md) has the route advice: they respawn on a per-player timer, so rotate 3–4 known spots rather than camping one.
- **Safeboxes and heists** — Refuges in the outlaw cities. Safeboxes get **Fortune's Favor's +50% gold**.
- **Pickpocketing** — **Light Fingers +50%** success, **Prowler's Talisman +5%**, **Cutpurse's Art** for loot quality. Take **Ember** as your companion for the extra gold, and note the standing warning: **she does not forgive getting caught stealing.** Her rapport is the cost of sloppiness.
- **Treasure chests** — **Locksmith +70%** on forcing locks, **Treasure Hunter** for quality, **Fortune's Favor** for gold.
- **The Nowhere Vault** — the one place this loadout is also the correct *content* loadout, since the Vault is puzzles, traps and stealth rather than combat. It has **three wings — Arcanum, Cursed Castle and Lunar Path** — and each hides one of the Vault's three Rumors; see the Rumors section below. Entry and key sources are in [Mythic Items](../shared/mythics.md#prowlers-talisman-yours-but-not-for-combat); **bring spare Wondrous Nowhere Keys** for the chests and locks inside, not just the one that opens the door.

## Rumors — the system behind half the key sources

**Rumors arrived with Update 51** and this page listed them as a key source without ever saying what they are. They're a **new quest system with no HUD help and no quest markers** — you find the quest yourself and follow clues. ArzyeL's write-up puts it bluntly: *"Rumors is a new quest system in ESO, that offers no HUD assistance or quest markers!"*

**How to start:** claim **"Rumors Abound"** free from the Crown Store, **or** go to the **Southern Docks in Daggerfall, Glenumbra** and speak to **Tilli the Gossip** — she gives an introductory quest and unlocks your first Rumor, **"The Berliel Heirloom"**.

**Three tiers**, easiest to hardest: **Whisper → Plot → Conspiracy.** At the time of writing there are **10 Whispers, 4 Plots and 4 Conspiracies**, and all three Nowhere Vault Rumors are **Conspiracies**.

**Finding them in the UI:** the Quest Journal has a **dedicated Rumors section**. Entries start **hidden**, and **hovering over one reveals a hint pointing to the location of its first clue** — that's your way in when you're stuck. Every lead and clue you uncover stays recorded there.

**What they pay:** *"upgrading materials, like Gems for The Prowler's Talisman, Pardon Edicts, Wondrous Nowhere Keys that you can use to access the Nowhere Vault and other valuable items."* So Rumors feed both halves of this page — they're a **key source** *and* a **gem source**.

!!! note "Two things not confirmed"
    No source I could reach states a **daily or weekly cap** on Rumors, or whether individual Rumors are **repeatable**. Also: this repo credits the **Gem of Deception** specifically to Rumors, which comes from itemlevel.net — ArzyeL confirms *gems for the Talisman* drop from Rumors but doesn't name that one. The link is sound; the exact gem name is the less certain half.

### The three Rumors inside the Nowhere Vault

These are the Vault's own Conspiracies, and they're what the Vault's big reward is gated behind. Each **starts** in a different wing:

| Rumor | Starts in | Room | What begins it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A Cage that Shines** | **Arcanum** | Hunt of the Watchers | Lure the only watcher — Watcher Filtration Gills |
| **A Cage that Shimmers** | **Cursed Castle** | Gallery of Curios | The **Artist's Lens** — see below |
| **A Cage that Sparkles** | **Lunar Path** | Moonlit Isles | Break it for the **Resonating Core** |

!!! warning "Correction — a Rumor does not stay in one wing"
    This page said "one per wing" on 2026-11-07. That's wrong, and it matters for planning a run: **a Rumor starts in one wing and finishes in another.** *A Cage that Shimmers* begins in the **Cursed Castle** and ends at **The Lakehouse in the Lunar Path wing**. So you can't clear a Rumor by clearing a single wing — expect to cross the Vault, which means budgeting keys for rooms in more than one wing. Fixed 2026-11-07.

**Where the three Timeward Seals are collected.** Each Rumor ends in the **next** wing's informational room — the wings rotate Arcanum → Cursed Castle → Lunar Path → Arcanum:

| Rumor | Starts in | Seal room | Wing | What you interact with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A Cage that Shines** | Arcanum | **Prison of the Unrepentant** | Cursed Castle | Subjugating Mindspore → **Physical Escape** |
| **A Cage that Shimmers** | Cursed Castle | **The Lakehouse** | Lunar Path | Connoisseur's Vision → **Creative Transcendence** |
| **A Cage that Sparkles** ⚠️ | Lunar Path | **Custodian's Repository** | Arcanum | Astralight Lumen → **an Astral Tear** |

*⚠️ The first two rows are sourced. The third is **inferred** — Custodian's Repository and the Astral Tear are the only informational room and mechanism left over, and the rotation pattern holds for the other two. I haven't read the Sparkles guide yet, so treat that row as a strong guess rather than a fact.*

**What the seals are for:** the **Ancient Direnni Quasigriff** mount sits in the **Sage's Trove** behind three locks, and **breaking a Timeward Seal there removes one**. All three gone, and you interact with the mount to collect it. The Trove itself opens by completing Vault rooms to repair the **Nexus Orrery**, which also completes the *Invitation to Treasures* quest.

**And each Rumor pays 5 Wondrous Nowhere Keys on completion** — so these part-fund the next run rather than only consuming keys.

So the Vault has **two** things worth finishing, not one: the **Gem of Cunning** for your Talisman, and the **Quasigriff**.

### The Artist's Lens, and the full Shimmers chain

The Lens is **found in the room, not brought into it** — this page named it without saying that:

1. In the **Gallery of Curios**, go to the **eastern** room and interact with **"The Destroyed Painting"**. Read the riddle it gives you.
2. **Collect the Artist's Lens lying near the painting.**
3. Go to the **southern** room and **use the Lens under the stairs**. That grants you **Artist's Touch** and starts *A Cage that Shimmers*.

*⚠️ The two sources disagree slightly on step 3: ArzyeL's dedicated Rumor guide says **"under the stairs"**, while its Nowhere Vault overview says **"above or below the stairs."** The dedicated guide is the better bet, but look both places. Whether the Lens is consumed on use isn't stated anywhere I could reach.*

The rest of the chain, since you'll want to know what you're committing to before you start it:

| # | Step | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Artist's Touch** | The Lens, above |
| 2 | **Carafe of Salt Water** | **Sinking Courtyard** — interact with the three **Rain Totems** to activate the **Brackish Fountain**, then the fountain itself. **The totems spawn in random spots every run** — see below |
| 3 | **Connoisseur's Vision** | **Haunted Undercroft** — *Alter the Stew* to bring out the **Stewmaster** boss, then kill her |
| 4 | **Timeward Seal** | **Lunar Path wing** → clear through to **The Lakehouse** → *Creative Transcendence* |
| 5 | **Break the seal** | **Sage's Trove** — this finishes the Rumor and pays **5 Wondrous Nowhere Keys** |

Note step 3 is the only **combat** step in the chain, and step 4 is the wing crossing. Nothing in it needs keys beyond the ones that open the rooms you pass through.

!!! warning "The Sinking Courtyard has two different sets of three things in it"
    This trips people up, so keep them separate:

    - **Three Rain Totems** — the **Rumor** objective. **Their locations are random every time**, so there's no map to memorise and no guide can tell you where to walk; you sweep the room looking for them. **You must have Artist's Touch active to interact with them at all** — if you can't see or use them, you skipped the Lens. **Order doesn't matter**: hit all three and the **Brackish Fountain** activates, then interact with it for the **Carafe of Salt Water**.
    - **Three crystals** — the **room** objective, nothing to do with the Rumor. You destroy them to be allowed to leave, and **they reform**, so move quickly between them.

    **And the room's hazard: don't linger on the sand.** Stand on it too long and you're **teleported back to the entrance** — which on a room you're sweeping for randomly-placed totems is the real time cost.

    *The fountain's position in the room isn't stated in any source I could reach, and neither is how the Sinking Courtyard is reached — it's one of the Cursed Castle wing's four rooms (Gallery of Curios, Sinking Courtyard, Haunted Undercroft, Prison of the Unrepentant), but no source gives the path or says whether the room is randomly rolled.*


### A Cage that Shines — the Arcanum chain

!!! danger "The one mistake that costs you the Rumor"
    **Hunt of the Watchers has a room objective and a Rumor objective, and finishing the room lets you leave before you've started the Rumor.** ArzyeL's guide says it outright: *"This completes the room but do not leave yet."*

    - **Room:** collect **five Seeker Glyph Keys** while avoiding the watchers, and open the sealed area. *(These are not Wondrous Nowhere Keys — the word "key" is doing two jobs in this vault.)*
    - **Rumor:** collect the **Shrill Whistle** in the area, then deal with the sigil-marked watcher.

**Starting it, in the Hunt of the Watchers:**

1. Clear the room (the five Seeker Glyph Keys), but **don't walk out**.
2. **Collect the Shrill Whistle** somewhere in the area. The guide also says to *"search for the sigil on one of the hills"* — it doesn't say whether that's the same sigil as the one over the watcher.
3. **Lure the one watcher with a sigil above him** to the **runic prison near the room's entrance**.
4. **Make him vulnerable, then kill him.** He drops the **Watcher Filtration Gills**, and that starts the Rumor.

*⚠️ The two sources disagree on step 4: the dedicated Rumor guide says **use the Shrill Whistle** when he enters the prison area; the Vault overview says **interact with the runic prison** itself. Try the whistle first — that's the dedicated guide, and it explains why you're told to pick the whistle up — and fall back to the prison. Unlike the Shimmers chain, **no starting buff is named** for this one.*

**The rest of the chain:**

| # | Step | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Watcher Filtration Gills** | Hunt of the Watchers | Above |
| 2 | **Fungal Sight** | **Murky Grotto** | With the Gills, interact with the **Toxic Bud**, then **stand on top of it and out-heal the poison**. **Deliberately don't kill the toxic plants, and leave a few enemies alive to drag the trial out** — that's what forces the **Toxic Matron** to appear. Kill her |
| 3 | **Subjugating Mindspore** | **Fungal Chasm** | Fungal Sight reveals **invisible stairs** on the left. Jump onto the green mist, follow it to a small side area, and interact with the Mindspore — the overview calls it "a secret area" |
| 4 | **Timeward Seal** | **Cursed Castle wing** → **Prison of the Unrepentant** | Clear the wing's challenge rooms to reach it. Entering activates the Mindspore; interact with **Physical Escape** for the seal |
| 5 | **Break the seal** | **Sage's Trove** | Finishes the Rumor and pays **5 Wondrous Nowhere Keys** |

**Two combat steps here, not one:** the sigil-marked watcher (unnamed in both sources) and the **Toxic Matron**. Shimmers only had the Stewmaster, so this is the fightier of the two.

**Nothing in this Rumor is described as randomly placed** — which is worth saying, because the Shimmers chain's Rain Totems are. No source mentions randomness for the watcher, the Glyph Keys, the Whistle, the Toxic Bud or the Mindspore.

!!! warning "The guide's Fungal Chasm tip doesn't apply to you"
    For the jumping in the Fungal Chasm, ArzyeL suggests **Streak** (*Storm Calling*) **via Subclassing**. **Both of your characters are pure class** — that's the whole reason you have Class Mastery — so that shortcut is off the table. Expect to do the Fungal Chasm on foot, and budget a little patience for it.

*Two more things the sources conflict or go quiet on: the **Physical Escape's** exact location (the Rumor guide says an adjacent room, the overview says the Prison room itself), and **which** of the Cursed Castle's three challenge rooms step 4 requires — the overview lists Gallery of Curios, Sinking Courtyard and Haunted Undercroft but doesn't say which the Rumor needs.*

## Bounty and Heat — the two meters people confuse

- **Heat** is short-term: it's why guards are currently interested in you. **Swiftly Forgotten** drops it by 64 every 3 seconds, and **Fade Away** wipes 25% of it when you escape a guard.
- **Bounty** is the gold price on your head. **Swiftly Forgotten** takes 115 off every 3 minutes, **Friends in Low Places** removes 1,000 once a day for free, and **Kickback** makes the bounty you *choose* to pay 40% cheaper.
- **Clemency** is the emergency button — once a day, a guard lets you go and keeps his hands off your stolen goods.
- **Timely Escape** is the other emergency button: in combat with a bounty, look for a Footpad to teleport you into a Refuge.

From the maintenance notes, unchanged: **HarvestMap + HarvestRoute** for routes and **Lost Treasure** for maps and troves. The console-safe equivalent is the Green tree above plus the Keen Eye passives.
