# Werewolf Cheat Sheet
### Pack Leader — the wolves fight for her, she stays alive

!!! warning "Update 51 made every werewolf button expensive — and six passive names on this page were wrong"
    **The costs, from the U51 notes:** Pounce **1,071 → 3,213 Stamina**, Roar **1,071 → 3,443**, Rip and Tear **765 → 1,800**, Claw Fury **383 → 420 per tick**. Hircine's Fortitude's heal came down **10%**. Nothing about her loop changes — Claw Fury is still the cheap button she lives on — but **Roar and Pounce are now one press per pack, not per rotation.**

    Everything here was re-read off **ESO-Hub's live Werewolf skill pages on 2026-09-30**, which fixed five of six passive names, Hircine's Fortitude's real numbers (it's better than this page said), and which morph Blood Hunger buffs. **In-game tooltips still win.**

Werewolf isn't a class — it's a transformation she slots on any character. She builds normally, puts the transformation ultimate on a bar, and presses it to turn into a beast with its own skill bar. With the **Pack Leader** morph it's about as easy and hard-to-die as ESO gets: two wolves fight on their own, she takes less damage, and one button heals her a ton.

## Morph: Pack Leader

Both morphs are of **Werewolf Transformation** (*World → Werewolf*). She takes **Pack Leader**:

- **Major Protection** (−10% damage taken), **+15% Block Mitigation**, and **Minor Courage** for her and anyone grouped with her
- **Summons two direwolves that fight for her** — free damage she doesn't have to manage, *and* they cut the cost of staying in wolf form by nearly two thirds (see below)
- **Enduring Rampage** — her version of the Rampage super adds **4,000 Health Recovery** on top of +15% damage, +20% movement speed, and **every werewolf ability costing nothing for 20 seconds**. It is *not* a button: it fires on its own once she's built **1,000 Fury** by dealing damage. Post-U51 that free-cast window is the best thing about being a wolf — it's when she should press Roar.

**The transformation ultimate also gives +15% Stamina Recovery just for sitting on her bar**, transformed or not.

## The werewolf form bar

When she transforms she gets a fixed 5-slot beast bar. Recommended slots:

1. **Feral Pounce** (morph of Pounce, *Werewolf*) — **dynamic**: pressed from far away it's the leap; pressed in melee range it's **Feral Carnage**, a **12-second** bleed that hits **harder and harder as the target's health drops** (up to +450%). Either way it gives her **200 Stamina back** on every damage tick, which is how she pays for the rest of the bar. She'll mostly use the melee half. **3,213 Stamina for the leap since U51** — so leap in once, then stay in.
    - **Feral** is the **single-target** morph (bosses — the default here). **Brutal** is the **AoE** morph, better if she's mainly clearing packs.
2. **Hircine's Fortitude** (morph of Hircine's Bounty, *Werewolf*) — **her panic button, and it's better than this sheet used to say.** It heals **5,890 scaling off her Max Health**, gives her **12% of her Stamina back** (up to double, the more health she has), and **while it's slotted she also gets Major Brutality (+20% Weapon and Spell Damage) *and* Major Vitality (+12% to every heal she receives)**. No downside. Costs 4,590 Magicka — her only Magicka cost in form.
    - **That Major Brutality matters for her specifically:** between Fortitude and Ferocious Roar's Major Savagery, her wolf bar buffs its own damage *and* crit with no potion and nothing scribed.
    - *Why not Hircine's Rage?* Rage is the other morph. It heals less (3,368), scales off offensive stats, and grants up to **12% damage done** — but it **raises the damage she takes by the same amount**, and it's weakest exactly when her health is already low. It does grant **Minor Berserk** while slotted (confirmed 2026-09-30, after this repo briefly recorded otherwise). On a sheet built around not dying, Fortitude still wins clearly: bigger heal, +12% healing received, no penalty.
3. **Ferocious Roar** (morph of Roar, *Werewolf*) — **fears** everything nearby for 4 seconds and leaves it **Off Balance for 7**, hands her **two free Blood Hunger stacks**, applies **Major Courage** to her and anyone nearby, and grants **Major Savagery while slotted** (her crit buff — no potion needed). **3,443 Stamina since U51 — the most expensive button on the bar.** One press per pack.
4. **Claw Fury** (morph of Rending Claws, *Werewolf* — the U50 rename of Infectious Claws) — a channelled claw swipe over **4.7 seconds** hitting up to 6 enemies. Her main damage and the cheap button she lives on: every second it **heals her 1,675 off her Max Health by itself** (Pale Order stacks on top), builds Fury, and adds a **Blood Hunger** stack worth **+25% damage**. **Hold it to the end** — see below. *(The other morph is **Bloodclaws**, the AoE version.)*
5. **Rip and Tear** (morph of **Gnash** — the U50 redesign of Piercing Howl, *Werewolf*) — **her spammable.** Applies **Major Breach for 15 seconds** (more penetration for everything she does) and the Sundered status effect, **heals her 3,414 off her Max Health**, and deals **125% more damage to anything under 25% health** — a built-in execute. 1,800 Stamina.
    - ⚠️ **Correction:** this sheet used to say Rip and Tear spends a Blood Hunger stack for **+25% damage**. It doesn't — the live tooltip says it spends the stack to make the **heal** 25% bigger. (It's **Bloody Gnash**, the other morph, that turns stacks into damage.) That suits her sheet fine — a bigger heal is what she wants — but the loop below is worded correctly now.
    - ⚠️ This sheet also claimed it **taunts while she's blocking**. That is not on the current tooltip and I couldn't source it, so **don't rely on it to hold aggro.** Flagged 2026-09-30.

### Blood Hunger — the one mechanic worth knowing

**Claw Fury adds a Blood Hunger stack every second she holds the channel, and each stack is +25% damage** — so letting it run all the way to the end matters a lot. **Ferocious Roar gives her two stacks outright.** **Rip and Tear then spends a stack to make its heal 25% bigger.** Hold the claws, *then* Rip and Tear.

⚠️ **"Prowl" — unverified.** This sheet used to say werewolf form has its own stealth and that pouncing out of it does **+50% damage and stuns**. There's no Prowl ability on ESO-Hub's Werewolf line and nothing in the U50 or U51 notes mentions it, so **don't plan an opener around it.** Flagged 2026-09-30.

## How to play it

1. Turn into a wolf (press the ultimate — it costs 100 Ultimate).
2. **Pounce in** once to close the gap → **Ferocious Roar** to scare them and grab two Blood Hunger stacks.
3. **Hold Claw Fury all the way through**, then **Rip and Tear**. That's the loop, and it's nearly all she should press — Claw Fury is the cheap button and it heals her while it runs.
4. **Hircine's Fortitude whenever she's hurt.**
5. The two wolves keep attacking on their own; she just holds Claw Fury.
6. **When Rampage fires** (it happens on its own, at 1,000 Fury) **everything is free for 20 seconds** — that's the moment to press Roar and Pounce again.
7. **Eat a corpse** (hold interact on a body) between fights — up to 4 seconds, and it heals her *and* refills the Ultimate that keeps her a wolf.

**Short version:** wolf out, the pets fight, hold Claw Fury → Rip and Tear, press Hircine's Fortitude when hurt, eat bodies between packs. Roar and Pounce once a pack — they're expensive now.

## Staying a werewolf — it costs Ultimate now

U50 removed the old drain timer. The form runs on Ultimate instead:

- **100 Ultimate to transform**, then **100 Ultimate every 10 seconds to stay in form**…
- …except the **Call of the Hunt** passive cuts that by 16%, plus another 16% for every transformed werewolf or direwolf grouped with her — **including herself and her two direwolves**. That's 64%, so her upkeep is **~36 per 10 seconds**. Her morph is by far the cheapest one to stay transformed in, and she gets much longer wolf time than a Berserker does.
- **If she and her husband both wolf out as Pack Leaders, they hit the passive's 80% cap** — two wolves plus four direwolves — and upkeep drops to about **20 per 10 seconds each**. At that point the form is close to permanent. Worth trying on a dungeon night.
- **Devouring a corpse restores Ultimate**, not time: up to 4 seconds, **15 Ultimate and a 3,200 heal per second** — so a full corpse is **60 Ultimate**, about 17 more seconds of form for her. Eating corpses is how she extends the form mid-fight. *(U51 pushed the Devour synergy to the bottom of the prompt list, so in a group she may not get offered it — hold interact on a body between packs instead.)*
- Nothing about attacking keeps the form alive any more — attacking builds **Fury** toward **Rampage**, which is a separate thing.

## Passives — buy all of them

*World → Werewolf line.* **Buy every werewolf passive** — there are only six and they're what keep the form strong and long. **Five of the names on this sheet were the pre-rework ones until 2026-09-30**; these are the live ones:

| Passive | What it does for her |
|---|---|
| **Insatiable Hunger** *(was listed as "Devour")* | Eat corpses: **15 Ultimate and a 3,200 heal per second**, up to 4 seconds |
| **Feral Cruelty** *(was "Savage Strength")* | **+20% Weapon and Spell Damage** *and* **Major Resolve** (+5,948 Armour) — a defence passive too, which this sheet didn't know |
| **Call of the Hunt** *(was "Call of the Pack")* | The upkeep discount above |
| **Master of the Chase** *(was "Pursuit")* | **+30% Movement Speed** and **+50% Stamina from Heavy Attacks** — her cheapest answer to the U51 costs |
| **Blood Rage** | **+10** Fury per cast, so Rampage comes sooner |
| **Shadow of the Bloodmoon** *(was "Bloodmoon")* | Lets her bite someone else **once a week** at the ritual site |

Buy Fighters Guild passives (Slayer) too.

*5× Order's Wrath (crafted — her husband makes it) + 5× Deadly Strike (jewelry + weapons + boots) + Slimecraw + Pale Order. Deadly Strike's 15% channelled-damage bonus buffs Claw Fury (a genuine channel — that's the whole reason for the set); Pale Order heals her from her own damage. She barely needs the optimal setup — Pack Leader's wolves + Major Protection carry overland and normal dungeons on their own. In-game tooltips override.*

*Why only one monster piece: Ring of the Pale Order takes the mythic slot, and 12 gear slots only stretch to 5 + 5 + 1 monster + 1 mythic. So the helm is a **pure stat line** — Slimecraw's 2-piece Minor Berserk can never fire here. Its 657 Critical Chance is the default because she already owns it. **Settled 2026-09-30, read off the live tooltips:** her wolf-form **Minor Berserk** comes from **Hircine's Rage**, not Ferocious Roar — and since this sheet runs **Hircine's Fortitude**, she does **not** have Minor Berserk in form. She gets **Major Brutality and Major Vitality** from Fortitude instead, which on this sheet is the better trade. Ferocious Roar gives Major Courage plus **Major Savagery while slotted**. See [Gear slot math](../shared/gear-math.md).*

## Champion Points — Spend Order to 1600

At CP 1250 she has ~417 points per color; ~533 at 1600. **Each table is in the order she actually unlocks it** — the tree opens outward from the center, so buy top to bottom and every row is reachable by the time she gets to it (connector stars come before the deeper stars they gate). The **`Slot`** line names the active stars for that tree — buy those as soon as the tree lets her reach them, and fill the passives as she paths through. Exact node adjacency shifts a little with which stars she picks, so glance at the in-game tree to confirm.

!!! tip "This table is a priority list, not the whole tree"
    **Warfare has 48 stars — 35 slottable, 13 passive.** Maxing all 13 Warfare passives costs **320 points**; four slottables cost 200. **Fitness has 41 stars — 28 slottable, 13 passive**, totalling **342 points** of passives. The table below names what matters most *for this build*, in the order you reach them. It is not the whole tree.

    **These Warfare passives are not in the table below, and two of them are large:**

    | Passive | Max | What it does |
    |---|---|---|
    | **War Mage** | 30 | **+100 Weapon and Spell Damage to Magical attacks** — Magic, Flame, Frost, Shock |
    | **Mighty** | 30 | **+100 Weapon and Spell Damage to Martial attacks** — Physical, Poison, Disease, Bleed |
    | **Flawless Ritual** | 40 | +30% per stage chance to apply a **Magical** status effect |
    | **Battle Mastery** | 40 | +30% per stage chance to apply a **Martial** status effect |

    Take **whichever of War Mage / Mighty matches your damage type**, plus the matching status-effect star. **For werewolf that's Mighty and Battle Mastery** — every werewolf ability deals Physical or Bleed damage, both Martial. (Compare the Dragonknights, which are Flame/Magical since U49 and want War Mage.)

    Fitness also leaves out **Tireless Guardian** (Block costs 20 less Stamina per stage, max 20), **Savage Defense** (Bash costs 45 less per stage, max 30) and **Fortification** (+2% damage blocked per stage, max 30) — all of which earn their place if you block a lot in melee.

    **The rule that follows: remaining passives outrank every "swap option" row.** A passive works every second of every fight; a fifth slottable does nothing at all unless you un-slot something for it. And because re-slotting is **free** out of combat and a full respec costs only **3,000 gold**, there is no reason to pre-buy a configuration you merely *might* want later — buy it when you need it.

    *Star costs and effects verified 2026-09-26 against esodecoded.com's datamined star pages.*

## Turning each other into werewolves

Once her husband (or anyone) is a werewolf, the **Bloodmoon** passive lets them **bite her** at the werewolf ritual site — so she doesn't have to farm the Werewolf world boss herself. Do the short Werewolf questline after infection to unlock the skill line.

## Companion

A **tank or healer companion** is a nice safety net while she learns to stay in form: **Isobel (Tank)** or **Sharp-as-Night (Healer)**. See `../shared/companions.md`.
