# Race & Class — Best Pairings

### The smallest dial in any build — read the caveat first

!!! warning "Race is the smallest dial you can turn — and the only one that costs real money"
    Across every build in this repo, **race is worth about a 5% spread** at the very most, and usually less. It costs a **Crown Store Race Change token** (real money) to switch. So the household rule is unchanged: **run whatever the character already is.** This page is for *new* characters, for curiosity, and for the one case where race genuinely matters — a chronic **sustain** problem. Do **not** re-roll an existing character for the damage numbers here; a couple of percent is noise by this repo's "kick ass and not die" standard.

Two ways to read this page: **best race for a class** (you picked the build, which race fits) and **class for a race** (you already are a race, which builds suit it). Both are below, plus a full passive reference at the bottom.

---

## The short version

Post-rework, every class is hybrid, so **race tracks your stat pool and your role — not your class.** That collapses the whole question to a handful of answers:

| You are… | Ceiling pick (most damage) | Household pick ("kick ass *and* not die") | Sustain fix (only if you have a problem) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Magicka DPS** | **High Elf** (Altmer) | **Khajiit** — nearly identical damage + crit healing + recovery + health | **Breton** (magicka) |
| **Stamina DPS** | **Dark Elf** (Dunmer) | **Khajiit** — top-tier crit damage that also survives | **Redguard** (stamina) |
| **Tanky / "just don't die"** | — | **Nord** (tankiest) or **Khajiit** (safe damage race) | **Imperial** or **Argonian** |

**If you remember one thing:** **Khajiit** is the household's default answer. Its +12% Critical Damage **and Critical Healing** (Feline Ambush), +915 to all three stats (Lunar Blessings), and flat recovery (Robustness) make it a damage race that also heals harder and lives longer — the exact trade this repo weights for. **Dark Elf** (stamina/flame/hybrid) and **High Elf** (magicka) edge it by a couple percent on a parse dummy and give up the survivability to do it.

## Class for race (reverse lookup)

Already committed to a race — yours, or a character you don't want to pay to change? Here's what each race is happiest doing. **None of these is a bad choice**; the spread is small, so a "worse" race for a role still clears all the content in these guides.

| If you're… | It's best at | Plays these builds well |
|---|---|---|
| **High Elf** (Altmer) | Magicka DPS ceiling | any magicka DPS — his Mag DK / Sorc / Necro, her magicka one-bars |
| **Dark Elf** (Dunmer) | Stamina DPS ceiling + **hybrid + flame** | any stamina DPS; especially flame builds (Mag DK's fire kit loves the flame-damage passive) — genuinely fine on *both* pools, the best "one race, any DPS" pick |
| **Khajiit** | Crit damage **+ survivability + sustain** | **everything** — the household all-rounder; the "don't die" damage race for either player, either pool |
| **Nord** | Tanking / not dying | any build you keep dying on; Werewolf; hard vet solo pushes |
| **Redguard** | **Stamina sustain** | stamina builds that run dry — Warden, Arcanist, stam NB/Templar, Werewolf; the one real answer to a stamina shortfall |
| **Breton** | Magicka sustain + spell resist | magicka DPS with a sustain problem; magicka PvP |
| **Imperial** | Hybrid sustain + survivability | tanky hybrid builds; anything where you want cost reduction on *both* pools |
| **Argonian** | Potion sustain + healing | survivability-first play; a build that leans on potions; off-healer duty |
| **Orc** (Orsimer) | Stamina bruiser (damage **and** toughness) | Werewolf, stamina PvP, stam DPS that also wants to be hard to kill |
| **Wood Elf** (Bosmer) | Stamina DPS with roll-dodge + sustain lean | stamina builds in dodge-heavy content; a lighter-sustain stamina DPS |

## Race passive reference (Update 50)

Combat-relevant passives only, in plain terms. **Exact values change rarely but do change — confirm the current numbers on [UESP](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Races) or your in-game character sheet.** Every race has a "+15% XP for [armor/weapon] type" passive too; those don't affect combat, so they're omitted.

| Race | Signature combat passives | One-line read |
|---|---|---|
| **High Elf** (Altmer) | Max Magicka; Weapon/Spell Damage; restore Magicka/Stamina on ability use; bonus Max Magicka with a Storm/Flame/Frost ability slotted | **Top magicka DPS.** Pure ceiling, no defensive help. |
| **Dark Elf** (Dunmer) | Max Magicka **and** Stamina; Weapon/Spell Damage; Flame Resistance **and** bonus Flame Damage | **Best stamina DPS, best hybrid, best flame.** The "any DPS" race. |
| **Khajiit** | +12% Critical Damage **and Critical Healing**; +915 Max Health/Magicka/Stamina; flat Health/Magicka/Stamina Recovery | **The all-rounder.** Damage that also heals and survives — the household pick. |
| **Nord** | Max Health & Stamina; increased Physical/Spell Resistance (armor); Ultimate generation when you take damage | **The tank race.** The answer to "I keep dying." |
| **Orc** (Orsimer) | Max Health & Stamina; Weapon/Spell Damage; Health Recovery & Healing Done; sprint speed/cost | **Stamina bruiser.** Damage *and* toughness; great PvP and Werewolf. |
| **Redguard** | Max Stamina; **restore Stamina on weapon-ability use**; reduced weapon-ability cost | **Stamina-sustain king.** The only race that truly fixes a stamina shortfall. |
| **Breton** | Max Magicka; increased Spell Resistance; Magicka Recovery; reduced Magicka ability cost | **Magicka sustain + spell resist.** The caster's Redguard. |
| **Imperial** | Max Health & Stamina; **restore Health/Stamina** on ability use; **−6% cost on all abilities**; Healing Done | **Hybrid sustain + survivability.** Cost reduction on both pools. |
| **Argonian** | Max Health & Magicka; **potions restore Health/Magicka/Stamina and hit harder**; Healing Done & Received; Disease/Poison resist | **Potion sustain + healing.** Survivability-first; leans on potions. |
| **Wood Elf** (Bosmer) | Max Stamina; Stamina Recovery; Weapon Critical; reduced roll-dodge cost; Poison/Disease resist | **Stamina DPS with sustain + dodge.** Nimble, forgiving stamina race. |

## Bottom line

1. **Don't pay to re-roll.** Whatever the character already is clears the content — race is ~5% at most.
2. **New character, want damage that survives?** **Khajiit.** It's the whole "kick ass and not die" thesis in one race.
3. **Chasing the last 2% on a parse?** **Dark Elf** for stamina, **High Elf** for magicka — knowing you're trading a little survivability for it.
4. **A specific character keeps running out of resources?** *That's* the one time race earns a change: **Redguard** (stamina) or **Breton** (magicka). But first rule out the real cause — his DK stamina issue is diagnosed as light-attack weaving gaps, not race.

*Sources: race hierarchy cross-checked against Hack the Minotaur, Alcast, Dottz Gaming, and The Tank Club race guides (2026); passive values per [UESP](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Races). In-game tooltips override every guide here. Revision: 2026-08-15.*
