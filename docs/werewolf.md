# Werewolf — Both of You
### One transformation, two setups: full (him) and easy (her)

!!! warning "The werewolf line was reworked in Update 50 — verify the bar in-game"
    ZOS renamed and redesigned several werewolf skills in U50 (the new **Claw Fury** channel is the headline). The names and effects below are checked against the current [Hyperioxes](https://hyperioxes.com/eso/dps/werewolf-build), [Alcast](https://alcasthq.com/eso-werewolf-build-pve/) and [ESO-Hub](https://eso-hub.com/en/guides/becoming-a-werewolf) werewolf builds — but **confirm the exact morphs on your own bar**, because in-game tooltips always win over any guide.

Werewolf isn't a class — it's a transformation you slot on **any** character. You build the character normally, put the Werewolf Transformation ultimate on a bar, and press it to turn into a beast with its own skill bar. It's melee, fast, self-healing, and (once you learn to stay in form) one of the most forgiving strong playstyles in the game — which is why it's great for **both** of you.

---

## Pick your morph — this is the whole difference between your two setups

Both morphs are of **Werewolf Transformation** (*World → Werewolf*). You only get one.

| | **Werewolf Berserker** (him) | **Pack Leader** (her) |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Damage | Survivability + easy |
| Grants | **Major Berserk** (+damage) + bonus Bleed on light/heavy attacks | **Major Protection** (−10% damage taken), Minor Courage, extra block mitigation, and **summons two Dire Wolves that fight for you** |
| Fury Rampage (the re-press) | More damage + speed, removes ability costs 20s | **Health recovery** instead of damage |
| Why it fits | You want the parse | The wolves do damage while she stays alive; nearly can't die |

**Her setup is Pack Leader.** Two wolves fighting on their own + Major Protection + a huge self-heal means she can facetank most overland/dungeon content while pressing very little. **His setup is Werewolf Berserker** for the damage.

## The werewolf form bar (shared)

When you transform you get a fixed 5-slot beast bar. Recommended slots — the abilities are the same for both of you; only the transformation morph above differs.

1. **Feral Pounce** (morph of Pounce, *Werewolf*) — gap-closer; restores 200 Stamina and builds **Fury** (which extends your time in form). Your engage.
2. **Hircine's Rage** (morph of Hircine's Bounty, *Werewolf*) — your **main self-heal** (scales off max health), builds Fury, and while slotted passively grants Major Brutality/Sorcery + Minor Berserk. The "don't die" button. *(Prefer pure defense? Hircine's Fortitude is the other morph.)*
3. **Ferocious Roar** (morph of Roar, *Werewolf*) — **fears** nearby enemies (panic CC + sets them Off Balance), applies **Major Courage**, and gives a Feeding Frenzy synergy (+6% damage, Minor Force). Big buff *and* an "everybody back off" button.
4. **Claw Fury** (morph of Infectious Claws, *Werewolf* — U50 rework) — a **channeled** claw attack, ~5 seconds in a cone; your main sustained damage. Because it's a channel, **Deadly Strike buffs it**, and with Pale Order + the Reaving Blows CP star it heals you while it ticks.
5. **Fifth slot — confirm in-game.** The reworked line leaves one flex slot (a Pounce or Infectious Claws morph, or Piercing Howl). Slot whichever the live [Hyperioxes werewolf build](https://hyperioxes.com/eso/dps/werewolf-build) currently uses — this is the one piece the rework makes worth checking on the bar rather than trusting a static list.

**Ultimate in form:** re-pressing the transformation triggers **Fury Rampage** (damage for him, health recovery for her).

## Staying a werewolf (the #1 thing new werewolves get wrong)

- **Out of combat, you stay a wolf forever.** The timer only drains in combat.
- **In combat, dealing damage and using skills builds Fury**, which keeps extending the form — so *keep attacking*, don't stand still.
- **Devour corpses** (hold the interact key on a dead body) to **heal AND add a big chunk of time** to the form. Between packs, snack.
- Passive **Blood Rage** extends the form when you take/deal damage; **Devour** is the heal-and-extend. Buy every werewolf passive (below).

---

## Passives — buy all of them

*World → Werewolf line.* Rule of thumb: **every werewolf passive, always.** They're what make the form strong and long.

- **Pursuit** — more stamina recovery in form
- **Blood Rage** — builds Fury (extends form) when you take damage
- **Bloodmoon** — lets you infect another player at the ritual site (that's how you'd turn *each other* — see below)
- **Devour** — eat corpses to heal and extend the form (your main between-fight sustain)
- **Savage Strength** — Weapon/Spell Damage while transformed, scaling with your missing-health… (big damage passive)
- **Call of the Pack** — reduces the ultimate cost to transform and the form's drain (stay a wolf longer)

*(Buy the Fighters Guild passives too — Slayer especially — since werewolves lean on those, and the transformation is boosted by time spent slotted.)*

## Gear

Werewolf can't bar-swap, so it's **one loadout**: your armor + jewelry + equipped weapons' set bonuses all apply in form. Both setups lean on **Deadly Strike** (it buffs the Claw Fury channel and your bleeds) and **Ring of the Pale Order** (heals you from your own damage — the don't-die mythic).

**Food:** any Max Health + Stamina dish (Orzorga's Smoked Bear Haunch)
**Attributes:** 64 Stamina (shift to Health if getting one-shot)
**Race:** whatever the character already is — the spread is ~5%

## How to play it

1. Pre-buff on your normal bars if you want, then **press the transformation ultimate**.
2. **Feral Pounce** onto the enemy → **Ferocious Roar** (fear + buff) → hold **Claw Fury** for damage.
3. **Hircine's Rage** whenever health dips — it heals hard.
4. **Keep attacking** to hold the form; **Devour a corpse** between packs to heal and add time.
5. Re-press the ultimate for **Fury Rampage** when you want the burst (him) or the health recovery (her).

**Her short version:** turn into a wolf, the two wolves fight, hold Claw Fury, press Hircine's Rage when hurt, eat bodies between fights.

## Turning each other into werewolves

You catch Lycanthropy from a bite (a Werewolf world-boss scratch in **Bangkorai / Reaper's March / The Rift** at night, or a bite from another player). Once one of you is a werewolf, the **Bloodmoon** passive lets that person **bite the other** at the werewolf ritual site — so you only need to get infected once, then you can turn each other. Do the short Werewolf questline after infection to unlock the skill line.
