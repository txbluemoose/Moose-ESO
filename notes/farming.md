# Farming — Gathering, Chests, Troves, Surveys

Non-combat loadout notes. Combat build barely matters here; CP and passives do the work.

## Green CP (the whole build)

**Slot:** Treasure Hunter • Master Gatherer • Steed's Blessing • Plentiful Harvest

**Buy (passive, no slot needed):** Rationer • Liquid Efficiency • Fortune's Favor • Gilded Fingers • Meticulous Disassembly • War Mount

## Passives to buy

- **Legerdemain** — Trafficker (faster fence resets), Finders Keepers, Light Fingers. Essential for troves and safeboxes.
- **Thieves Guild** — Haggling and the rest; raises fence payouts
- **Crafting lines' Keen Eye passives** (Blacksmithing/Clothing/Woodworking) — makes nodes visible from much further away. Biggest single time-saver in resource farming.

## Gear

- **Night's Silence** (crafted) — removes the sneak movement-speed penalty. The core thief piece.
- **Night Mother's Embrace** (crafted) — reduced detection radius and sneak cost
- **Prowler's Talisman** (mythic) — 5% pickpocket, crouch-invisibility once per 45s. The husband owns it, partially shard-upgraded. Not for combat (competes with Pale Order).
- **Ring of the Wild Hunt** if owned — out-of-combat movement speed

Medium armor is the stealth weight (sneak cost and stealth movement passives).

## Addons (PC only)

- **HarvestMap** — records every node/chest/fishing spot, pins them, heatmap mode, respawn filtering, and a **route planner**
- **HarvestMap-Data** — pre-populated community node locations
- **HarvestRoute** — turns a route into live turn-by-turn navigation; auto-inserts newly found nodes into the tour optimally; SmartPath reduces zigzagging
- **Lost Treasure** — puts survey and treasure map dig sites directly on the map
- **Minion** — addon manager

Limitation: the addon API exposes harvest-node respawn state but **not container state**, so chest pins show all known locations regardless of whether one is currently there.

Console has none of this — green CP stars and Keen Eye passives are the whole toolkit.

## Route tips

- Do all of a zone's treasure and survey maps in one trip
- Thieves troves respawn on a per-player timer; rotate 3–4 known spots rather than camping one
- For chest runs in unfamiliar zones, HarvestMap's Farming Tour routes around impassable terrain (the cliff-top chest problem)
